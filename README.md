## The image

A giant ocean wave rears up and becomes a horse, its mane made of
white sea foam, while a giant exploding orange sun blazes at the
crest of the wave. Inside the barrel of the wave rides one tiny
surfer in a red wetsuit — a small human heartbeat at the centre of
a force of nature. No text, no words: the picture does the talking.

The idea is a single jolt: scale, surprise and momentum in one
frame. A wave that is also a horse, a sun that is also an
explosion, and a surfer small enough to make the whole scene feel
enormous. It reads at a glance, even small, because the horse's
silhouette and the sun are the whole composition.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) copies `entry.jpg` to
`/output/entry.png`. It is a plain byte copy — the image is never
decoded or re-encoded — so both runs of the program write exactly
the same bytes.

Run it:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
